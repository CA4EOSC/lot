- **Satellite monitoring:** Sentinel-1 (ESA, free), ALOS-2 (JAXA), and commercial SAR providers (SatSense, Sixense, InSAR Corp) support mm-precision ground movement monitoring.
- **Research preprints:** Research Square, arXiv, and EGUSphere host the latest climate-adjusted shrink-swell risk modelling studies.
