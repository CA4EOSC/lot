# Shrink-Swell Risks Summary

## Risks

- Ground subsidence from drying and shrinking expansive clay soils
- Ground heave from rewetting and swelling clay soils
- Cracking and movement of building foundations
- Damage to walls, floors, extensions, and utilities
- Road, rail, highway, tunnel, and pipeline deformation
- Tree- and vegetation-driven soil-moisture changes near buildings
- Drought-driven increases in subsidence claims and losses
- Climate-change-driven expansion and intensification of risk zones
- Risks to water infrastructure and buried assets
- Construction risk where site soils are not adequately assessed
- Insurance affordability, exclusions, and protection gaps
- Uncertain localized exposure without detailed soil, geology, and monitoring data

## Key risk drivers

Expansive clay mineralogy, soil-moisture cycles, drought, rainfall and recharge, vegetation, foundation design, and local geology.
